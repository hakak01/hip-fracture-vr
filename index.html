About

Training simulator for a trochanteric (AO 31A1) hip fracture: reduction and DHS guidewire. Not for clinical use.

CT data: case from the TotalSegmentator dataset v2.0.1 (Wasserthal J. et al. Radiology: Artificial Intelligence 2023;5(5):e230024). Licence CC BY 4.0. Changed: cropped, resampled, bone and skin surfaces extracted. doi:10.5281/zenodo.10047292

3D engine: three.js (MIT licence).

Browser: drag to turn, pinch or scroll to zoom, two fingers to pan. Tap the C-arm screen buttons or use the bar below.
